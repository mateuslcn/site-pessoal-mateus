AI Adoption at Scale

Many organizations face a common hurdle: AI pilots that fail to translate into clear business value. Teams get caught up in superficial usage metrics rather than real, scaled engineering outcomes. I work with organizations to establish secure governance, structured patterns, and sustainable AI adoption models.

"The goal isn't just utilizing AI tools, it's maintaining the rigorous software engineering discipline that creates real impact."

By focusing on cultural adaptation alongside technology implementation, we build structures where developers collaborate safely with AI to deliver clean, maintainable, and high-impact software systems.

What I Bring

  • RPI Coaching for Agentic Coding: Implementing Research, Plan, and Implement frameworks specifically tailored for AI-driven development.
  • Organizational Governance: Empowering collaborators across all levels and functions to design, build, and orchestrate their own robust, interconnected internal systems using agentic AI.
  • Practical Developer Up-skilling: Training engineering teams in prompt precision and agentic system orchestration.

Software Development

A successful development pipeline relies on aligning human dynamics with technical rigor. I consult on development lifecycle optimization, agile engineering practices, testing standards, and organizational restructuring that removes friction.

"High-performing engineering teams are built by designing transparent communication channels and clear ownership boundaries."

I help organizations streamline execution, shorten time-to-market, and implement metrics that accurately reflect product stability.

What I Bring

  • Process Optimization: Refining legacy structures and integrating AI-assisted workflows to support fast-paced, high-quality deployments.
  • Quality & Test Alignment: Incorporating automated verification pipelines, CI/CD, and AI-augmented testing standards.
  • Empathetic Team Scaling: Establishing psychological safety, clear ownership, and effective collaboration in hybrid human-AI teams.

Engineering Management

Managing multi-disciplinary engineering projects requires aligning technical precision across diverse domains. I coordinate high-performance teams to design, plan, and execute engineering workloads that integrate digital solutions with physical infrastructure.

"Effective management bridges the gap between hardware, software, and physical construction to deliver unified systems."

I ensure that complex projects are delivered on time and within scope by enforcing strict quality assurance, regulatory compliance, and cross-functional collaboration throughout the project lifecycle.

What I Bring

  • Digital Products & Software: Extensive experience directing software projects, lifecycle development, and AI integrations for scalable systems running in production 24 hours per day, 7 days per week (24/7).
  • Electrification & Utilities: Managing electrical power distribution systems, grid connections, and electrification infrastructure workloads.
  • Civil Works & Operations: Planning, coordinating, and supervising site operations, construction compliance, and structural engineering layouts.

Career Mentoring

Career progression is a deliberate engineering problem. It requires clear models, behavioral awareness, and active communication to build authority and step into senior leadership roles.

"Professional acceleration is about expanding your sphere of influence and communicating technical excellence to business stakeholders."

I mentor individual contributors making the transition to management, coaching them on team leadership, behavioral communication, and strategic career moves.

What I Bring

  • Leadership Onboarding: Guiding tech professionals as they transition into delegation, feedback, and mid management roles in AI-driven environments.
  • Strategic Intellectual Property & Writing: Leveraging technical writing, patents, publications, and AI-era intellectual assets as tools for global visibility.
  • Assertive Communication: Preparing professionals to navigate critical corporate conversations and alignments.

Engineering Classes

I deliver comprehensive classes and training sessions designed for students, researchers, and professional teams. My teaching spans foundational engineering subjects, specialized technical areas, software architecture, and modern management frameworks.

"Education in engineering is about building a solid foundation in basic sciences while continuously mastering modern design, control, and management practices."

My classes are tailored to bridge academic theory and industry reality, ensuring a clear path from mathematical modeling to practical operational application.

What I Bring

  • Basic Cycle & Programming: Instruction in fundamental engineering disciplines, including Calculus, linear algebra, and introductory programming/logic.
  • Electronics & Control Systems: Specialized topics covering analog and digital electronics, instrumentation, and control system design/stability.
  • Software Architecture: Practical classes on software engineering design patterns, system architecture, AI-assisted coding practices, and clean code principles.
  • Modern Project Management: Training in advanced agile frameworks, AI-augmented management tools, Management 3.0 principles, and hybrid project methodologies.

The Artist & The Story

Meet My Mom Conceição Costa

A retired public health social worker based in João Pessoa, Brazil, my mom (66) found in art a sanctuary for her emotions and a source of quality of life. Her hands, which spent decades dedicated to caring for others, now give life to vibrant oil canvases, delicate crochet, and intricate patchwork, as her retired life beautifully merges into nuances of color, texture, and form.

"In art, I rest my emotions and find inspiration to live."
